
# BRIDGE — SHAREHOLDER TRANSITION PROPOSAL

**A new trustless bridge structure for the Qubic ecosystem.**

---

## PURPOSE

This document presents the proposed shareholder / IPO transition framework, initial bridge scope, security approach and governance process. Implementation remains subject to approval.

### IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The proposal establishes a new trustless bridge structure with new smart contracts, infrastructure and operational responsibility.

The existing VOTTUN Bridge is **not** taken over technically or operationally, and the new structure does **not** assume any VOTTUN existing liabilities, contracts or obligations.

The proposal does, however, include a transition of the existing VOTTUN shareholder base into the new structure, as described in the shareholder / IPO framework below.

*For shareholder discussion / voting*

---

## 01 Purpose & Transition

**Objective:** Establish a new bridge project with its own smart contracts, governance and operational responsibility.

The NEW BRIDGE is a separate, independently operated project. It does **not** take over or operate the existing VOTTUN Bridge and does **not** assume any of its existing liabilities, contracts or obligations.

The transition addressed by this proposal concerns the shareholder / community structure and the creation of the new bridge framework.

The relevant shareholder vote(s) include acceptance of this proposal and the formation of the new shareholder structure. A Special off-chain IPO is part of the proposed transition.

## 02 Shareholder / IPO Structure

The proposed structure uses the Qubic IPO mechanism as the fundraising and distribution framework. The allocation is designed around a voluntary 1:1 community-support mechanism.

| Position                        | Allocation / Position | Purpose                                              |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Existing VOTTUN SC shareholders | 676                   | 1:1 voluntary community-support allocation           |
| New IPO participants            | 676                   | New bridge participation via the Qubic IPO mechanism |
| **Total**                       | **1,352**             | Proposed shareholder structure                       |

### 1:1 ALLOCATION

The 1:1 allocation is a voluntary community-support measure. It is **not** repayment, compensation, a liability assumption, a recovery guarantee, or a VOTTUN Bridge takeover.

The resulting NEW BRIDGE positions replace the former VOTTUN shareholder positions within the new structure. The former VOTTUN positions are retired / rendered unusable following the approved transition.

### Special off-chain IPO Auction

The standard Qubic IPO auction system is proposed to be replicated and adapted for this special situation and used once for the Special off-chain IPO through a Dutch Auction mechanism, subject to development and technical implementation by the development team. The final technical implementation and execution remain subject to development, testing and approval.

IPO funds are **not** automatically burned. They remain available for subsequent allocation through shareholder governance.

## 03 Support for Persons with Lost Personal Collateral

Support discussions focus on persons who actually lost personal collateral in connection with the bridge incident. The relevant reference is the person’s documented and verified personal collateral loss.

The affected users are real individuals, and the losses have had significant personal consequences. For some individuals, the lost funds represented a substantial portion of their personal savings or retirement funds.

The eligible collateral amount shall be defined by a snapshot at the time of the bridge incident. This snapshot determines the amount eligible for consideration and its corresponding wUSDT reference value.

Applicants seeking support may be required to provide a police report or other appropriate official documentation evidencing the reported loss, subject to verification and shareholder-approved procedures.

**Approved support shall be paid in QUBIC, based on the current value of the pair QUBIC/wUSDT, and on that snapshot reference value.**

### PROPOSED SUPPORT LEVEL

25%, 50%, 75% or up to 100% of actually lost and verified personal collateral may be considered. The final support level must be determined and approved by shareholder vote, based on available funds after the other approved and required costs have been considered.

After the required costs and approved allocations have been covered, shareholders may consider whether additional support is possible. There is **no** obligation to provide support beyond the amount approved through shareholder governance, and **no** automatic repayment, compensation or recovery guarantee is created. The support mechanism is **not** an assumption of any VOTTUN liability or obligation.

## 04 Bridge Scope

| Phase              | Scope                                                      |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Phase 1            | Ethereum (ETH) + BNB Smart Chain (BSC)                     |
| Phase 2            | Solana and additional networks if requested / approved     |
| Initial assets     | wUSDT, wETH and wBTC                                       |
| Later consideration| Additional bridge functionality and networks, subject to readiness and approval |

Phase 1 supports two-way bridging for the initial EVM assets (wUSDT, wETH and wBTC). Qubic-native asset-OUT functionality is planned for a later phase.

The bridge is intended to support two-way bridging of the initial EVM assets and interaction with trading, swaps and other smart contracts.

Additional EVM chains may be added later if requested and approved.

---

## 05 Trustless Design & Security

The new bridge is designed around a trustless architecture, reducing reliance on a central operator through on-chain verification, quorum-based oracle reporting and ZK-based proof verification.

The proposed risk controls are intended to limit exposure while the system is being tested and audited.

| Control          | Proposed approach                                              |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Initial TVL      | Start with defined TVL limits rather than opening at unrestricted scale |
| Gradual scaling  | Increase TVL progressively as testing and audits advance       |
| Emergency control| Pause / resume authority for a designated arbitrator           |
| Bridge structure | No multiple locking / vault-wallet structure for the free / dynamic bridge |

A designated Arbitrator, such as CFB or a member of the Qubic Core Tech Team, may be appointed to coordinate emergency pause and resume decisions when required by the security framework.

Shareholders are responsible for approved bug-bounty programs, audits and bridge governance.

---

## 06 ZK Prover Operation

The initial ZK-Prover team has the exclusive right for the first 12 months to operate the ZK prover infrastructure and generate ZK proofs for the bridge.

During this period, the prover portion of the approved fee distribution is directed to the operating ZK-Prover team.

The initial ZK-Prover team commits to operate the ZK prover for a minimum period of one year. Its role is limited to prover operation and proof generation and does **not** constitute ownership or control of the NEW BRIDGE shareholder structure.

If the designated ZK-Prover team abandons or ceases operation, a new ZK-Prover may be appointed so that bridge operation can continue subject to the applicable governance and security requirements.

After the initial 12-month period, shareholder governance may decide on continuation, replacement or further operation of the ZK-Prover team.

## 08 Shareholder Governance & Representatives

The current shareholders are to elect and formally legitimize a new Shareholder Representatives group to facilitate the transition toward decentralized shareholder governance.

The Shareholder Representatives act solely as decentralized community representatives and supporters of the NEW BRIDGE shareholders. They do **not** constitute a legal entity and do **not** assume any legal, contractual, financial or operational obligations of the NEW BRIDGE or the former VOTTUN Bridge.

The representatives serve as a communication channel between shareholders, the development team and the broader NEW BRIDGE community. They may help coordinate the transition, communicate shareholder requirements and communicate development and governance updates.

The governance structures and voting mechanisms described in this proposal are proposals for consideration, not final rules. The newly elected Shareholder Representatives shall review, discuss and further develop these proposals with shareholders and relevant technical participants. Final rules, procedures and parameters remain subject to shareholder approval.

The shareholder vote includes the election and formal legitimization of the new Shareholder Representatives.

## 09 Technical & Operational Structure

- The NEW BRIDGE smart-contract system is an independent project with new code, governance parameters and operational rules.
- Phase 1 target chains: Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain.
- Phase 1 assets: wUSDT, wETH and wBTC.
- Additional networks and functionality may be added later subject to readiness and approval.
- Existing infrastructure or code components may be used only if the development team considers them necessary for the implementation of the NEW BRIDGE.

Any reuse of existing infrastructure or code does **not** constitute a takeover of the former VOTTUN Bridge or an assumption of its liabilities, contracts or obligations.

The current Qubic VOTTUN Bridge is no longer operating; this proposal therefore concerns a new bridge structure, not a continuation of the existing bridge.

## 10 UI/UX & User Experience

The intended user experience is simple and requires only a few steps.

- UI/UX is intended to be integrated through relevant Qubic ecosystem interfaces where technically appropriate.
- Users should be able to connect a wallet, select the asset and destination, and complete the bridge process with minimal steps.

Discussions with ecosystem interfaces and community-operated DEX providers are already underway, with initial commitments already indicated.

Final UI/UX remains subject to technical feasibility, security requirements and governance approval.

## 11 Smart-Contract Development

The NEW BRIDGE smart-contract structure is proposed to be developed and deployed at **$0 cost** to the shareholder structure. The development team’s contribution includes development and gifting of the NEW BRIDGE smart-contract structure to the new shareholder structure, without development/deployment fees.

The smart-contract system remains an independent NEW-BRIDGE structure with its own code, governance and operational rules.

Existing infrastructure or code components may be used only if the development team considers them necessary for the implementation of the NEW BRIDGE.

Development and deployment remain subject to testing, security validation, governance approval and the applicable Computor Proposal before operation.

## 12 Bridge Fees & Revenue Distribution

| Item               | Proposed value                                      |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Deposit Fee        | 0%                                                  |
| Minimum Deposit    | None                                                |
| Withdrawal pricing | Dynamic BID / ASK-style mechanism                   |
| Revenue basis      | Bridge usage / transaction activity rather than asset value |

The withdrawal mechanism is intended as a market-driven pricing system: higher offers can receive higher execution priority, while inadequate offers may remain unfulfilled.

### Net withdrawal-fee distribution

| Recipient   | Share |
|-------------|-------|
| ZK Prover   | 50%   |
| Shareholders| 50%   |
| Burn        | 0%    |
| **TOTAL**   | **100%** |

The ZK Prover fee is dynamic and can influence transaction prioritization and execution speed. The OM fee is a separate deposit-stage fee. The BRIDGE SC receives the remaining amount after OM and ZK fees. The final fee schedule must be approved before implementation.

## 13 Fundraising & Post-IPO Governance

After the IPO, shareholders will vote on the distribution of raised funds. The exact allocation may depend on the amount raised and the approved governance decision.

| Category                                      | Proposed purpose                                                                 |
|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Support for verified personal collateral loss | Subject to the support mechanism and shareholder approval described in Section 03 |
| Bug bounties                                  | Strongly prioritized security bounty programs                                    |
| Required audit & security updates             | Required independent security audit(s), security / maintenance work and necessary updates |
| Remaining funds                               | Allocated according to shareholder vote and ideas proposed by the new shareholder team |

The additional IPO funds should strongly prioritize the required security audit and bug-bounty programs. Any remaining funds may be allocated according to shareholder vote and ideas proposed by the new shareholder team.

After the required costs and approved allocations have been covered, shareholders may consider whether additional support is possible. There is **no** obligation to provide support beyond the amount approved through shareholder governance.

IPO funds are **not** automatically burned. They remain available for subsequent allocation through shareholder governance.

The designated key holder is proposed to be CFB or a member of the Qubic Core Tech Team, subject to shareholder approval.

## 14 Governance Voting & Decision Process

The governance mechanisms below are proposals for consideration and further development by the elected Shareholder Representatives, shareholders and relevant technical participants.

- Voting via message signing
- Wallet Connect / wallet-based authentication
- A 1 QU voting transaction from a wallet holding the relevant shareholder position may be used
- Multi-epoch voting periods
- Multiple voting options
- Transparent vote counting

An alternative governance application may be used if preferred. Interim solutions may be necessary while the governance system is being implemented.

The elected Shareholder Representatives shall review, discuss and further develop the governance proposals with shareholders and relevant technical participants. Final procedures remain subject to shareholder approval.

---

## 15 Security, Audits & Controlled Scaling

Security validation and audits are required before full deployment. The project is intended to begin with defined TVL limits and to scale progressively as testing and audits advance.

- Bug-bounty programs may be funded through approved governance allocations.
- Required independent security audits and security / maintenance work may be funded through approved allocations.
- Further development may be funded through approved allocations.
- Emergency pause / resume procedures may be coordinated by the designated Arbitrator within the approved security framework.

These controls are intended to provide a controlled transition from testing to broader operation.

---

## 16 Future Scope

Solana and additional networks may be introduced in later phases if requested, technically ready and approved through the applicable governance process.

---

## 17 Final Principle — Independent NEW BRIDGE Structure

### INDEPENDENCE

The NEW BRIDGE is structured as an independent bridge project with new smart contracts, governance and operational rules. The former VOTTUN Bridge is **not** taken over, operated or assumed by the NEW BRIDGE, and **no** VOTTUN liabilities, contracts or obligations are transferred to the new project.

The shareholder / IPO transition is intended to create a transparent ownership structure, decentralized governance and a usage-based revenue model while maintaining controlled security and deployment conditions.

All material implementation parameters remain subject to the applicable shareholder governance process and the required Computor Proposal.

---

## 18 Final Cleanup

Remaining unconverted VOTTUN shareholder positions shall be retired to prevent future misuse or confusion.

A proposal may be submitted to Computors.

This document consolidates the proposed transition framework, bridge scope, security approach, governance process and fee model for shareholder discussion and approval.
